3. Sensitive conversations

Conversations with inyo are personal. You may choose to discuss your emotional wellbeing, relationships, mental health, or other sensitive topics. We treat all conversation content with heightened care:

Please only share what you are comfortable sharing. You can request deletion of your conversation history at any time by emailing joininyo@gmail.com.
