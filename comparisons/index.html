Edit 01

Favicon rebuilt from brand mark

Original tab icon was the black wave + “inyo” wordmark. Changed to the wave mark alone on Inyo’s yellow circle (#f5ff66). Chat avatar uses a higher-fidelity composite from the real header logo.

Edit 02

Chat simulation profile pictures

In the phone texting demos, the Inyo PFP was missing/broken. Replaced with the yellow-circle logo mark (derived from the real header logo, not the simplified favicon path), sized consistently across phones.

Edit 03

Logo fades over content

The fixed centered logo was covering headlines while scrolling. Instead of moving it top-left, it now fades to ~14% opacity when it sits over page content.

Edit 04

Legal page titles

Framer left an unsubstituted {{Title}} placeholder in the document title / social meta. Fixed to “Privacy Policy — inyo” and “Terms & Conditions — inyo” in exact/ and site/ (original left untouched).

Note: Framer client hydration can briefly rewrite the tab title back to {{Title}} at runtime; the HTML source in exact/legal/ and site/legal/ is corrected.
